Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect basic information such as your name and email address. When you use Spendly, we also store the expense records you enter — including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We do not collect payment information, government IDs, or any sensitive personal data beyond what is needed to run the service.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide you with a personalized expense tracking experience. This includes displaying your dashboard, generating spending summaries, and allowing you to filter and manage your records. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the service for you.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely and access is limited to your account. We take reasonable precautions to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or loss. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal data with third parties. We may use minimal infrastructure services (such as hosting providers) to operate the app, but these providers do not have access to your expense records and are bound by confidentiality obligations.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, you can reach us at support@spendly.app. We will respond to all privacy-related inquiries within 5 business days.
